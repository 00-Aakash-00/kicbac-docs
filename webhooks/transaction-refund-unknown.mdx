sidebarTitle: "refund.unknown"
description: "Sent when a refund outcome cannot be confirmed. Reconcile the transaction before refunding again."
openapi: "openapi/kicbac.openapi.json webhook transaction.refund.unknown"
---

The `transaction.refund.unknown` event reports an ambiguous refund outcome. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Do not refund again automatically. Query and reconcile the existing `transaction_id` or `order_id` so a successful but unconfirmed refund is not duplicated.
