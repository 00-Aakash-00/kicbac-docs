title: "transaction.credit.success webhook reference"
sidebarTitle: "credit.success"
description: "Sent when a standalone credit succeeds. Record the credit and avoid submitting it again."
openapi: "openapi/kicbac.openapi.json webhook transaction.credit.success"
---

The `transaction.credit.success` event reports that a standalone credit was accepted successfully. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Record the credited amount and transaction reference in your ledger. Do not submit the same credit again when this event is redelivered.
