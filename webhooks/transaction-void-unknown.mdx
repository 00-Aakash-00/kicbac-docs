The `transaction.void.unknown` event reports an ambiguous void outcome. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Do not void or refund automatically. Query and reconcile the existing `transaction_id` or `order_id` before choosing the next action.
