sidebarTitle: "credit.failure"
description: "Sent when a standalone credit fails. Reconcile its reference before creating another credit."
openapi: "openapi/kicbac.openapi.json webhook transaction.credit.failure"
---

The `transaction.credit.failure` event reports that a standalone credit was unsuccessful. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Do not post the amount as credited. Record the response details and query the existing `transaction_id` or `order_id` before deciding whether to create another credit.
