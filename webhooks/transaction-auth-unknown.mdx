---

The `transaction.auth.unknown` event reports an ambiguous authorization outcome. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Do not capture or authorize again automatically. Query and reconcile the existing `transaction_id` or `order_id` before deciding whether a new authorization is safe.
