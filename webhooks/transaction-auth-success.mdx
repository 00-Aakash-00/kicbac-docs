The `transaction.auth.success` event reports that a card authorization was approved. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Record the authorization and `transaction_id` before any later capture or void. An authorization is not a settled payment, so keep fulfilment aligned with your capture policy.
