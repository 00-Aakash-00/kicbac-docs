sidebarTitle: "capture.failure"
description: "Sent when a capture fails. Reconcile the authorization before attempting another capture."
openapi: "openapi/kicbac.openapi.json webhook transaction.capture.failure"
---

The `transaction.capture.failure` event reports that a capture was unsuccessful. Verify the signature over the raw request body before parsing, then deduplicate deliveries by `event_id`.

Do not mark the payment as captured. Record the response details and query the existing `transaction_id` or `order_id` before deciding whether another capture is appropriate.
